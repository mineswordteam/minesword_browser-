# Privacy Policy & Features - Minesword Browser

## Incognito / Private Mode
- Zero disk persistence (History, Cookies, Caches, and LocalStorage are purged or kept in volatile memory only).
- Third-party cookies disabled during incognito sessions.

## Data Control
- Users can clear history, bookmarks, and local site state at any time via Settings.
- Zero analytics or telemetry sent to third-party servers.
